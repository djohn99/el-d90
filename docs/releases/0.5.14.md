## What’s new in 0.5.14

- **Cluster setup** starts with a choice between AWS SSO and kubeconfig import. Saved AWS profiles and diagnostics are available in collapsible sections. [#15](https://github.com/djohn99/el-d90/issues/15)
- **AWS SSO** guides you through browser sign-in, account, role and cluster selection. It shows the current step, clears the previous role and cluster when you change accounts, and offers retry when loading fails. [#14](https://github.com/djohn99/el-d90/issues/14)
- **Searchable pickers** for AWS accounts, roles and namespaces open over the form without shifting it. Select with the keyboard, press Escape, or click outside to close. [#13](https://github.com/djohn99/el-d90/issues/13)

Both downloads are public. Drag the Apple Silicon app to Applications. On Windows x64, extract the entire ZIP before opening **el-d90.exe**.
